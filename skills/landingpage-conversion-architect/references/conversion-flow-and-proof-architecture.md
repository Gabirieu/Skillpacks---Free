# Conversion Flow And Proof Architecture

Use this reference when a landing page must guide a visitor from first impression to action with very little attention budget.

## Key decisions

- Define the target audience, core promise, primary CTA, and most dangerous objection before building sections.
- Choose whether the page sells an offer, a product category, a lead capture, or a product qualification step.
- Decide what proof matters most: customer logos, metrics, screenshots, testimonials, guarantees, or expert credibility.

## Execution rules

- Make the above-the-fold area answer what it is, who it is for, why it matters, and what to do next.
- Place proof near moments of doubt instead of isolating it in a decorative social-proof block.
- Use section order to reduce friction: pain, promise, mechanism, proof, objections, CTA.

## Quality bar

- A first-time visitor should not need to scroll far to understand the offer and next step.
- Every section should earn its place in the conversion narrative rather than existing as filler.
