# Checkout Conversion UX Architect Checklist

## Before changing anything

- Confirm scope, owners, dependencies, and constraints.
- Identify where **checkout flow confidence and clarity** is already defined or failing.
- Check current evidence, telemetry, documents, or user complaints.

## During design or audit

- Make tradeoffs around **validation and recovery design** explicit.
- Validate operational consequences for **conversion-safe UX sequencing**.
- Keep rollback, fallback, and communication plans visible.

## Before closing

- Summarize the decision in plain language.
- Attach validation, rollout, and risk notes.
- Assign owners and next steps.
